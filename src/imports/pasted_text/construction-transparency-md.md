The Problem
Building a home is a major investment, but homeowners often cannot be physically present to understand what is happening on their construction site.
A homeowner may be occupied with office work, business, travel, family responsibilities, or other commitments. They cannot be available 24/7 to visit the site, coordinate with contractors, check progress, verify completed work, or follow up on issues.
At the same time, construction involves multiple stakeholders — construction companies, builders, contractors, subcontractors, supervisors, and site teams — often working across different activities and stages.
This creates a communication and visibility gap.
Homeowners may depend on phone calls, WhatsApp messages, photos, spreadsheets, or occasional site visits to understand the current status of their project. Important information can become scattered, delayed, difficult to verify, or dependent on one person's updates.
The UX Challenge
How might we create a simple, transparent and connected construction experience that allows homeowners to stay informed without being physically present, while helping construction teams efficiently manage and document the work happening on site?

The Core User Problem
For Homeowners
“I can't be at my construction site all the time. I need to know what is happening, what has been completed, what needs attention, and whether the project is progressing as expected — without constantly calling someone or visiting the site.”
For Construction Companies & Builders
“We need a structured way to manage projects, coordinate teams, document progress, and keep homeowners informed without spending excessive time on manual updates.”
For Contractors & Subcontractors
“We need a clear understanding of what work needs to be done, what has been completed, what issues exist, and what is expected next.”
For Supervisors & Site Teams
“We need an easy way to record daily site activity, capture evidence of work, coordinate people, and keep the project record up to date.”

The Opportunity
This creates an opportunity for Houzeify to become the connection between the construction site and everyone who depends on it.
Instead of asking:
“What happened at the site today?”
Houzeify can make the answer visible through a structured digital record:
Who worked → What was done → What was completed → What issues occurred → Photos/Evidence → Current Stage → What's next

01 — What you want to build
Houzeify is a digital construction management and transparency platform that brings the entire construction project into one connected system.
It helps construction companies and project teams manage projects, coordinate site teams, track daily progress, manage tasks and issues, maintain construction records, and eventually give customers a clear view of what is happening on their project.
At its core:
Project → People → Work → Progress → Evidence → Record → Customer

02 — Who it is for
Houzeify is designed for everyone involved in a construction project, with different experiences for each stakeholder.
Primary users
🏗️ Construction companies
👷 Builders & contractors
🧑‍💼 Project managers
👷 Site supervisors
🔨 Site workforce & trade teams
Project stakeholders
🏠 Homeowners / customers
📋 Architects & consultants
🤝 Subcontractors and specialized teams
The key idea is that Houzeify connects the company, project team, site and customer instead of treating them as separate systems.

03 — What it does
Houzeify helps teams manage the construction lifecycle through connected modules:
Projects
Create and manage construction projects with project details, stages and timelines.
Construction Stages
Organize work around the project's construction stages.
Daily Progress
Record what happened on site each day with progress updates and photos.
Tasks & Issues
Assign work, track pending tasks, identify issues and monitor resolution.
Workforce & Site Team
Know who is actually working on each project and what role/trade they perform.
Documents & Bill of Quantities
Maintain project documentation and construction cost/quantity information.
Customer Transparency
Give customers visibility into project progress, updates, photos and records.
Live Site & Time-lapse
Provide a visual view of the construction site and its progress over time.
Reports & Construction Records
Turn project activity into structured construction records and reports.
Hozie Construction AI
Eventually use the accumulated project data to provide intelligent construction assistance, insights and recommendations.

04 — What it looks like
The experience should feel like a modern SaaS platform built specifically for construction — clean, visual, structured and easy to understand.
The visual language should communicate:
Professional + Construction-focused + Data-driven + Human + Trustworthy
The company experience centers around a Project Workspace, where teams can move between:
Overview → Progress → Timeline → Tasks → Issues → Workforce → Live Site → Documents → Bill of Quantities → Team → Customer → Reports
The customer experience is much simpler:
My Project → Progress → Timeline → Photos → Live Site → Documents → Questions
